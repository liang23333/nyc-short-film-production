# New York City Filming Permits & Public Street Regulations Guide

This guide details municipal permitting rules, legal exemptions, insurance requirements, sidewalk logistics, and jurisdictional boundaries for filming on public property in New York City, governed by the **Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment (MOME)** / **Office of Film, Theatre and Broadcasting (OFTB)**.

---

## 1. Do You Need a Permit? The MOME Decision Matrix

Under Title 43, Chapter 9 of the Rules of the City of New York (RCNY §§ 9-01 and 9-02), filming on City sidewalks and exterior public streets does **not** always require a permit.

### Scenario A: Filming WITHOUT a Permit (Handheld / Tripod Exemption)
You do **NOT** need a film permit if your production meets **ALL** of the following criteria:
1. **Equipment**: You use only handheld cameras, cameras mounted on a tripod, handheld props, and handheld equipment (e.g., boom pole, portable sound bag, handheld bounce board).
2. **Non-Exclusive Use**: You do not claim exclusive use of city property (you cannot rope off sidewalks, close streets, or block pedestrian flow).
3. **No Special Elements**: You do not use prop weapons, prop emergency vehicles, fire/smoke effects, stunts, or actors dressed in police, fire, or postal uniforms.
4. **No Production Parking**: You do not request reserved parking privileges for production vehicles (personal cars, SUVs, or mini-vans parked legally at meters or public spots do not trigger this).
5. **No City Personnel Needed**: You do not require dedicated NYPD Movie/TV Unit officers or FDNY fire safety personnel.

> **Important**: Standing on a public sidewalk or bridge walkway with a camera on a tripod without blocking pedestrians is a constitutionally protected First Amendment activity recognized by NYC law.

### Scenario B: When a Permit IS Legally Required
You **MUST** obtain a standard MOME Film Permit if you:
- Use equipment beyond a handheld camera and tripod (e.g., C-stands, dolly tracks, camera jibs/cranes, generators, external light fixtures on stands, cables running across sidewalks).
- Assert exclusive use of public space (e.g., closing a sidewalk, directing pedestrians to wait or cross the street).
- Request reserved curb parking for production trucks or grip vans.
- Include stunts, simulated violence, prop guns, fake blood, or actors in NYPD uniform.
- Use pyrotechnics, open flame, smoke effects, or water towers.

## 2. Letter in Lieu of Permit (LiLOP)

For productions qualifying for the no-permit exemption, MOME offers a free, downloadable **Letter in Lieu of Permit (LiLOP)**.
- **Purpose**: Official document issued by the Mayor's Office affirming that small-footprint shoots (handheld/tripod) are lawful under city code.
- **Best Practice**: Print copies of the LiLOP and keep them in the production binder and director's kit on set. If approached by NYPD patrol officers, park rangers, or curious property managers, presenting the LiLOP resolves disputes immediately without delay.

---

## 3. Standard MOME Permit Application & Fees

If the shoot requires stands, lighting, or reserved curb space:
- **Application Fee**: **$500 non-refundable fee** covers a consecutive **14-day production period**.
- **Application Portal**: MOME Film Office online permitting system.
- **Lead Time**: Applications must be submitted at least **48 to 72 hours (minimum 2 full business days)** before call time. For street closures, pyrotechnics, or structural rigging, 10–14 business days are required.
- **Fee Waiver Requests**: Low-budget indie and student filmmakers can petition for a fee waiver on grounds of financial hardship.
  - Requires: Letter of Intent (1–2 pages explaining project and hardship), detailed Project Budget, and Budget Narrative.
  - Turnaround: Requires 3 business days for MOME review before permit filing.

## 4. Insurance Requirements (Mandatory for Permitted Shoots)

If a standard film permit is filed, proof of insurance is mandatory:
- **Commercial General Liability (CGL)**:
  - Minimum coverage: **$1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate**.
  - Policy format: Must be **occurrence-based** (claims-made policies are not accepted) on standard ISO form CG 00 01 or broader.
- **Additional Insured Endorsement**:
  - The Certificate of Insurance (COI) must explicitly designate:  
    `"The City of New York, including its officials and employees"` as an **Additional Insured**.
- **Certificate Holder Address**:
  - *City of New York, Mayor's Office of Media & Entertainment, 1 Centre Street, 26th Floor, New York, NY 10007* (or 1697 Broadway, 6th Floor).
- **Automobile Liability**: Minimum $1,000,000 combined single limit if production vehicles are parked in permitted spaces.

## 5. Street & Sidewalk Regulations on Shoot Day

1. **Pedestrian Clearance (ADA Compliance)**:
   - A clear, unobstructed path of at least **4 to 8 feet** must remain open on the sidewalk at all times.
   - You may not obstruct building entrances, wheelchair ramps, subway station stairwells, or fire hydrants.
2. **Cable Management**:
   - No loose cables may cross pedestrian walkways. All lines must be taped down with heavy-duty gaffer tape and covered with ADA-compliant yellow jacket rubber cable bridges.
3. **Community Notification Flyers**:
   - If holding a permitted shoot or parking vehicles, productions must post and hand-deliver **Filming Notices** to all residents and commercial tenants on the block **24 to 48 hours in advance**.
   - The notice must specify project title, production company contact, shoot date/hours, and brief description of scenes.
4. **Noise Code (NYC Noise Control Code § 24-218)**:
   - Amplified music, bullhorns, or loud generator operations between **10:00 PM and 7:00 AM** are strictly regulated and require special nighttime variances.

## 6. Multi-Agency Jurisdictional Boundaries

Filming in NYC often crosses into specialized jurisdictions that require separate permissions in addition to or instead of MOME:

| Location Type | Governing Body | Permitting Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **City Streets & Sidewalks** | MOME (Mayor's Office) | Standard MOME Permit or LiLOP exemption. |
| **NYC Public Parks** (Central Park, Washington Sq, etc.) | NYC Parks Dept & MOME | NYC Parks Media Approval (MPA) required prior to MOME permit. Hourly fees may apply. |
| **Subways, Buses, MTA Bridges** | MTA Arts & Design | MTA permit required; non-refundable application fee; proof of specialized railroad insurance. |
| **City Hall, Municipal Buildings, Courts** | DCAS (Citywide Admin Services) | DCAS filming permit ($3,200 administrative fee + monitor fees). |
| **Drones / Aerial Filming** | NYPD & NYC DOT | NYC Administrative Code § 10-126 requires NYPD Unmanned Aircraft Permit ($150 fee, Part 107 license, FAA waiver). Drones cannot fly over people/moving traffic. |
| **State Parks** (e.g., Gantry Plaza, Marsha P. Johnson) | NYS Parks, Recreation & Historic Pres. | NY State Parks filming permit required. |
| **Federal Parks** (e.g., Statue of Liberty, Gateway) | National Park Service (NPS) | Special Use Permit via NPS. |
