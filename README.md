## Regulatory Compliance Summary
1. **Permit Exemption (Handheld/Tripod)**: If shooting with minimal equipment (handheld camera or tripod, handheld bounce board/boom mic, no reserved parking, no exclusive street use, no prop weapons/uniforms), no permit is required under MOME rules § 9-01. A free **Letter in Lieu of Permit (LiLOP)** can be carried on set.
2. **Standard MOME Permit**: Required if deploying lighting stands, C-stands, generators, cables on sidewalks, roping off sidewalk space, requesting production parking, or using prop weapons/stunts. Fee: $500 per 14-day window.
3. **Insurance Requirement**: If a permit is pulled, a $1,000,000 occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate Commercial General Liability (CGL) policy naming the *City of New York* as Additional Insured is mandatory.
4. **Sidewalk Clearance & Community Notice**: A minimum 4–8 ft clear path for pedestrians must be preserved at all times (ADA compliance). Community notification flyers must be distributed 24–48 hours prior to residential/commercial blocks.
